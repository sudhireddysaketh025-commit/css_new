Welcome

Hardik Pandya is an Indian international cricketer who currently captains the Mumbai Indians in the Indian Premier League (IPL) and plays as a premier fast-bowling all-rounder for the Indian National Cricket Team. Born on October 11, 1993, in Gujarat, he is celebrated as one of the finest white-ball all-rounders globally. He bats right-handed in the middle-order and bowls right-arm fast-medium pace.
